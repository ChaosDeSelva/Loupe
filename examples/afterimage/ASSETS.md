# Afterimage asset provenance

All raster game art in this example was created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on 2026-10-15, from the prompts below. No reference images, stock libraries, or another game's assets were supplied. Original generated PNGs are included; alpha transparency is preserved. Backgrounds are intentionally opaque. Grid atlases were visually reviewed for cell separation.

The project makes its generated assets available under its MIT license to the extent of rights held. This is a reuse permission, not a claim of exclusivity or trademark clearance. Code, procedural effects, and the three musical compositions are also MIT. Third-party font/runtime notices remain intact.

Music: **Glasslight** (100 BPM), **Violet Current** (108 BPM), **Ember Orbit** (116 BPM). Original stereo synthesizer compositions, no samples or recordings. The composer is `tools/compose_music.py`; OGG files are runtime exports. The sound effects are synthesized in `src/audio.lua`.

The illustrated sprite set covers Kestrel, drone, weaver, gunner, Quiet Engine, five equipment/pickup icons, laser and burst atlases. Vector mode preserves code-native art. AI artwork is not required to reuse Loupe or the simulation.
