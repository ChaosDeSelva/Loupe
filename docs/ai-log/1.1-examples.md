# 1.1: Example games upgrade (asteroids, platformer)

- Date: 2026-10-09
- Author: Claude Code (Opus 5.5), working in an isolated worktree off `v1.0.0`; reviewer: maintainer
- Status: done in code, **not run in LÖVE** (see "What was not verified")

## What was built
Scope was `examples/asteroids/**`, `examples/platformer/**` and a new `examples/shared/`. Nothing
under `loupe/`, `spec/`, `tools/` or the docs changed, and the examples use only the documented
`loupe.watch` / `loupe.attach` / `loupe.setOpen` API.

Both examples:
- `conf.lua`: `highdpi`, `resizable`, minimum window size, `msaa = 4`.
- F11 and Alt+Enter toggle desktop fullscreen (`examples/shared/window.lua`), then refit the layout
  straight away instead of waiting for `love.resize`. None of these keys clash with loupe's
  defaults (F1, P, `.`, `[`, `]`, `1`, Ctrl/Cmd+Z/Y/S). M mutes.
- Sound effects synthesized into `SoundData` at load (`examples/shared/sfx.lua`), so there are
  still no asset files. If audio can't be set up, the game prints why and keeps running.
- `love.draw` is wrapped in `push("all")`/`pop()`, so blend mode, line join and font changes
  don't carry over into loupe's overlay.
- Particles are plain tables (`examples/shared/particles.lua`), kept out of the watched game
  state on purpose: hundreds of short-lived specks would flood the Objects window and the
  picker. Their counts and strengths are what's exposed.
- `--smoke [frames]` is unchanged: it plays immediately (asteroids now skips its new title
  screen in smoke runs), opens loupe, and quits with code 0.

Asteroids:
- The playfield is the window. On a resize or fullscreen toggle, `game.w/h` update and rocks,
  bullets and the ship fold back into the new field. Objects that straddle an edge are drawn on
  both sides, and collisions measure to the nearest wrapped copy.
- Glowing vector look: additive halo passes under a bright core line (no canvas or shader, so
  HiDPI needs nothing special). Filled hulls and rocks hide the stars behind them.
- Particle explosions (streaks plus spinning shards), shock rings, a thruster trail, score
  popups, trauma-based screen shake (noise, squared), a white flash when the ship dies.
- Parallax starfield (3 depths, reacts to ship velocity) and a soft nebula.
- 3 lives with respawn and a spawn shield, an extra life every 10,000 points, a calm pause
  between waves with a "WAVE n" banner, a title screen, game over, and a session best score.
- Same roots as before (`game`, `rockSizes`, `bullets`) plus `fx` and `sound`. `game.ship`,
  `game.rocks`, `game.bullets`, `rocks.defs`, and `bullets.speed`/`bullets.life` are kept.

Platformer:
- Split into `player`, `enemies`, `camera`, `backdrop`, `hud`, `text`, `aabb`; `world` keeps the
  level and the rules. `view` is unchanged except for `getScale()`.
- Movement: acceleration and friction, coyote time, jump buffering (on the key press), variable
  jump height (`jumpCut`), heavier fall gravity, squash and stretch around the feet.
- Camera: exponential smoothing plus a look-ahead that swings with facing, still clamped.
  `pickable` still excludes `game.camera`.
- Parallax: sky gradient (a 4-vertex mesh), sun glow, drifting clouds, three procedural ridges,
  the nearest with trees.
- Coins bob, spin and twinkle; collecting one bursts sparks, shows "+1", and plays a chime that
  rises in pitch. Enemies walk with swinging legs, can be stomped (bounce), and kill on contact
  from the side. The flag waves, raises on arrival, and triggers confetti and fireworks plus a
  "LEVEL COMPLETE" panel with coins, time and falls.
- Text is drawn at the real on-screen size and scaled down into the virtual view, so it stays
  sharp when letterboxed up to fullscreen.
- `game.spawn` is a plain `x, y` table, so it shows as a draggable marker in loupe.

## Numbers
~2,500 lines across the two examples and `examples/shared` (up from ~465). `tools/check.sh`
passes: StyLua clean, luacheck 0 warnings / 0 errors in 77 files, 330 specs, coverage
`core/` 99.68%, `ui/` 99.60%.

## How it was verified (without LÖVE)
LÖVE was deliberately not launched (another agent was using it). Instead:
1. **luacheck parses every file** (0 warnings), so the syntax is valid for the LuaJIT standard
   library.
2. **A headless harness** (scratch, outside the repo) runs each real `main.lua` and `conf.lua`
   under LuaJIT with a *validating* `love` stub. Its `love.graphics` rejects non-finite or
   missing numbers, odd or short polygon vertex lists, unknown blend modes and line joins, and
   bad print arguments, and checks that every frame leaves push/pop balanced and the blend mode
   back on `alpha`. loupe itself is replaced by a recorder, which checks the watched roots and
   the `attach` options.
   - Asteroids (about 3,700 frames): smoke quits with 0 after exactly N frames and opens loupe;
     title, then Enter; Space does nothing on the title; Alt+Enter goes fullscreen without
     restarting; F11 goes back; the playfield follows 1728x1117, 500x340 and 960x640 with
     everything inside; thrust and fire; the `maxSpeed` clamp; a large rock scores 20 and splits
     into 2; a wrapped collision; death, then lives 2, then a respawn at the center with a
     shield; game over after 3 deaths with no respawn; Enter and R restart; the calm pause, then
     wave 2 with 5 rocks; the extra life; extreme tunables (`glow` 0 and 1.5, `glowPasses` 0 and
     5.5, `stars` 0 and 1000, `maxParticles` 0, a large rock with `r = 0`); mute; 3,000 frames of
     scripted input.
   - Platformer (about 3,900 frames): smoke; `pickable(camera) == false`; `toWorld(toScreen(p))`
     round-trips in windowed, fullscreen and a tall 400x900 window; F11 and Alt+Enter don't
     start the game from the title; tap jump about 22px vs held jump about 86px; a buffered jump
     fires on landing; a coyote jump works 3 frames after leaving a ledge, and doesn't with
     `coyoteTime = 0`; a pit death followed by a respawn; coin pickup; a stomp removes the enemy
     and bounces; a side hit kills; the camera stays clamped and leads; the win fires once, the
     timer stops, fireworks play, and Enter plays again; R restarts; extreme tunables; 3,000
     frames of scripted input.
3. **Sound levels** were measured offline: every preset stays within [-1, 1] (the two
   explosions saturate on a few dozen samples, on purpose), and all 12 synthesize in about 3 ms.

## What the checks caught
1. **The v1.0 level wasn't fully playable.** The harness measured a peak jump of about 58px
   (`jump = 330`, `gravity = 900`), but the first floating ledge is 70px up, and with no
   jump-cut the old jump was the same height. `jump` is now 400 (peak about 86px), which reaches
   every ledge, including the 80px one at x = 760.
2. Coin positions generated by the old `200 + (i % 3) * 30` formula would have put a new coin
   inside a new platform. Coins now have explicit, hand-placed positions.
3. The test's "fired" assertion first failed because bullets from the scripted burst hit rocks
   right away (the rocks had just been folded into a 500x340 field). The test was wrong, not
   the game; it now accepts a hit as proof of firing.

## What was not verified (the lead should check in LÖVE)
- **Nothing has been seen on screen.** The look (glow strength, colors, text placement and
  sizes, parallax, squash amounts) is unreviewed. The values are first guesses.
- That `love.audio` works on the CI runner (`SDL_AUDIODRIVER=dummy` plus xvfb). Failure is
  caught and printed, but whether `love` with no audio device reaches `M.load` at all is
  untested.
- `msaa = 4` on xvfb/llvmpipe. LÖVE is expected to fall back if it's unsupported.
- Real HiDPI on a Retina display, and whether `love.resize` fires after `setFullscreen` (the code
  refits either way).
- Frame rate: asteroids issues about 170 polygon draws a frame (glow passes) and the platformer
  about 300 (ridge strips). That should be fine for LÖVE 11's batching, but it hasn't been
  measured.
- That loupe's real overlay looks right on top: picking alignment while the screen shakes
  (shake offsets the drawing but not `toWorld`, so outlines lag by a few pixels during a shake),
  and Inspector editing of the new color tables.

## Known limitations
- Screen shake isn't part of `toWorld`/`toScreen`, so outlines are off by up to `shakeStrength`
  pixels while a shake lasts. Pause first, as for any moving object.
- Asteroids' best score is per session (not saved).
- The examples now `require("examples.shared.*")` through the repo-root `package.path` trick. A
  game copied out of the repo needs those files copied too, as noted in the main.lua comment.
