## What was built
- Tooling: `stylua.toml`, `.luacheckrc` (LuaJIT std, per-folder globals), `.busted`, `.luacov`.
- `tools/check.sh`: runs every gate. `tools/coverage_gate.lua` enforces per-folder thresholds.
- `spec/support/love_stub.lua`: a fake `love` that records graphics calls, with a headless mode.
- `loupe/init.lua`: version stub only.
- CI: gates on LuaJIT and Lua 5.1, plus a real-LÖVE smoke job under xvfb. Claude review workflow on PRs.
- Process docs: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/AI.md`, `docs/roadmap.md`, three ADRs, `docs/manual-test.md`.
- `examples/asteroids`: a complete small game in plain-LÖVE style, with a `--smoke N` flag.

## Test-first evidence
`spec/loupe_spec.lua` was written before `loupe/init.lua` and failed with "module 'loupe' not
found". It went green after the stub was added.

## What the gates caught
- **StyLua** rejected a 104-column `print` in `tools/coverage_gate.lua` that the AI wrote over
  the 100-column limit it had just configured. Fixed by running `stylua`.
- luacheck: 0 warnings on the first run.

## Environment issues
- macOS has no `timeout` command; the first smoke attempt failed with exit 127. Fixed by relying
  on the game's own `--smoke` flag to exit.
- busted, luacheck and luacov were installed with `luarocks --lua-version=5.1` against LuaJIT so
  local tests run on the same VM as LÖVE.
